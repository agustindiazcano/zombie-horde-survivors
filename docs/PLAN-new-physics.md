Decisions of 2026-11-01 (the author): (1) **all gravity is his own new one** (Hooke + inverse Archimedes effect, technique 8); (2) the **switch** `physics.engine` with `"classic"` until the new engine is ready; (3) **boxes stay on Rapier** until the rest is finished, then they move into the own solver (no 4.4 MB chunk just for crates): **option A**, a box as 8 spheres at the corners joined by rigid distance constraints like the ragdoll bones (real 3D rotation, stacking on the same Hooke and Coulomb maths); option B (a simplified AABB/OBB collider) only for crates that slide and never tip. Static prop boxes are already AABBs in `world.ts`. (4) **Gravity law confirmed** (PHYSICS section 8, rows 11 and 12; Plummer discarded). (5) The switch is `physics.engine` with `"classic"` (default) and `"diaz-cano"`; Map 12 and Map 13 (or a per-map or command-line override) switch it on for tests; the default flips only after units, blasts and ragdolls pass determinism and speed.
