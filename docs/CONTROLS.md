# Controls

The single reference for every key and mouse action in the game, the menus and the map editor. The README and CLAUDE.md link here instead of repeating it.

**Rule:** whenever a key or mouse action is added, moved or removed, change this file in the same commit. Nothing else in the repo lists controls.

## Direct control

You control one soldier; the rest of the squad follows and shoots on its own.

| Key / mouse                         | Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| W / A / S / D                       | Move (relative to where the camera looks)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Mouse                               | Aim (see _Views_ below)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Left click                          | Fire: hold for automatic guns (rifle, machine gun); one shot per click for semi-automatic ones (the pistol)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| R                                   | Reload the gun in hand from the ammo in the backpack (takes the gun's reload time; no fire meanwhile; the pistol always has spare magazines)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| F                                   | Melee: strike the nearest enemy in front (knife if held, else the gun's butt, else fists), shove it back and stagger it; shares the cooldown with firing and interrupts a reload. AI soldiers do it by themselves when an enemy gets very close                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Right mouse button (hold)           | Aim down the sights, in third and first person only: best accuracy and less recoil, walking pace only, a narrower view; sprinting, being hit, going down or changing view (T) cancels it. Settings (O) can switch it to click on / click off                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| E (action key)                      | Uses the closest thing in reach, like Half-Life: opens a crate, opens / closes a door or flips a wall switch, climbs into a machine-gun nest or a watchtower (or out of it), feeds rounds from your backpack to a turret or post that is running low (a locked door opens if you carry its keycard; a powered door only follows its switch; sliding and sci-fi doors open by themselves when you come near), enters or leaves a vehicle, lifts an object (crate, barrel, boulder), drags a fallen ally or a dead enemy (a corpse lies 45 s; a corpse only wins when nothing else is close) or takes an item lying on the ground. Holding a load or dragging a body, it lets go. Lifting needs strength: you lift your strength x 4 kilos (a body weighs 70, a boulder 70, a barrel about 30, a crate 18); too heavy shows the strength needed. A lifted load slows you and you cannot shoot; click throws it |
| Left click, while holding an object | Throw it at the aim point (heavier things fly shorter). It hurts and staggers the enemy it lands on, and breaks after a few throws. A shot at a fuel or explosive barrel sets it off                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Ctrl (or **C**)                     | The whole squad crouches: tighter spread, +10% damage, half speed (piloting a helicopter: descend; swimming: dive)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Z                                   | Hold to walk: the whole squad walks at a slower pace with the walk animation (crouch and sprint win over it)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Shift                               | The whole squad sprints (1.6x speed) while it has stamina; each unit tires on its own, then recovers (piloting an aircraft: fly faster; swimming: swim faster)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Space                               | Jump (only the soldier you control); walking into a box, it vaults up onto it (30 stamina) (piloting a helicopter: climb; swimming: come up)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Q                                   | Throw a grenade at the aim point (the cursor in top-down, the crosshair in third / first person)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| (no key) Head and critical hits     | In third / first person put the crosshair on an enemy's head for extra damage; in top-down a head hit is a small chance. Critical hits are random everywhere. Orange number = head, yellow = critical; the crosshair glows and a ping sounds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| G                                   | Drag a downed or dead ally next to you (half speed, one-handed guns only); press again to let go. **E** does the same                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| V                                   | Next to a vehicle, nest or tower: climb in; inside, step out (the driver leaving takes the crew out too). **E** does the same                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| T                                   | Change view: normal → third person → first person → normal                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Y                                   | Go back to RTS mode                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Mouse wheel                         | Zoom (normal and third-person views)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Right-drag                          | Rotate and tilt the camera (normal view only)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Ctrl+W closes the browser tab and the browser won't let a page block it, so **C** is provided as an alternative way to crouch.

## Time of day (maps with the `timeControl` rule, e.g. Map 11)

**.** (period) moves the clock forward and **,** (comma) back, a quarter of an hour per press (hold to run). The sun, sky and light follow; the hint line shows the time.

## Swimming and diving (maps with the `swim` rule, e.g. Map 10)

On these maps water is not a wall. Deeper than 0.9 m a soldier swims instead of walking (no stances, no jump; the squad follows at the surface). The keys keep their place: **C** / **Ctrl** dives deeper (down to 8 m, never below the sea floor), **Space** swims up, **Shift** swims faster. Under 0.5 m the head is under water: the **OXYGEN** bar (above the health bar) runs down over 30 s, the unit cannot shoot and swims a little slower. With no air left it loses 12 health a second; at the surface the bar refills.

## Vehicles, aircraft and boats

In a vehicle the driver steers with W / A / S / D and passengers keep shooting; zombies bite the hull. **V** or **E** climbs in or out (RTS: right-click a vehicle to send the selected soldiers in).

| Vehicle                                                   | How it works                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Jeep**                                                  | Arcade driving on land; runs enemies over above 5 m/s                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Speedboat**                                             | Driven like a jeep but only on water: the shore and the hulls of ships stop it. Climb in from the carrier's deck or a beach (reach about 6 m). You can step out only where dry ground is within about 8 m, never out at sea                                                                                                                             |
| **Helicopter, Huey, Chinook, V-22** (direct control)      | On the ground **hold W** (any move key) for 10 s to start the engine (the hint shows the percentage; letting go makes it fall back). Then **Space** climbs, **C** / **Ctrl** descends to the ground, **W / A / S / D** fly (only above 2 m), **Shift** flies faster. You can only get out when it is on the ground. Zombies cannot reach it above 2.5 m |
| **Aircraft in RTS** (pilot selected, or controlled by AI) | A move order (right-click) starts the engine by itself (3-4 s), climbs to cruise height, flies there and brakes on arrival. **Space** takes off, **C** lands and shuts the engine down, **H** switches what it does with no destination: hover (default) or fly circles                                                                                 |
| **Aircraft carrier, frigate**                             | Fixed ships: they cannot be entered or driven yet. The carrier's deck can be walked on and helicopters stand on it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Views (T)

| View                  | Camera                               | Aiming                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Normal** (top-down) | Above and behind, follows the unit   | The soldier aims at the cursor on the ground. Wheel zooms, right-drag rotates / tilts.           |
| **Third person**      | Over the shoulder, a bit above       | The mouse turns the view 1:1 and instantly; the crosshair in the centre is the aim. Wheel zooms. |
| **First person**      | At the soldier's eyes (model hidden) | Same as third person; the camera lowers when crouching and rises when jumping.                   |

In third and first person the cursor is locked (so you can keep turning) and W / A / S / D move relative to where you look. **Esc** releases the cursor and goes back to the normal view. The view also returns to normal if you switch to RTS mode, the controlled soldier dies, or the game ends.

## RTS mode

| Key / mouse                                   | Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| W / A / S / D                                 | Pan the camera                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Left click                                    | Select a soldier; over a thing the cursor labels (see below) it sends the closest selected soldier to use it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Left-drag                                     | Box select (hold **Shift** to add to the selection)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Right click                                   | Send the selected units to that point, or to use the labelled thing under the cursor (a right _drag_ rotates the camera instead)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| F                                             | Selected units follow the controlled soldier again                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| G                                             | Aim a grenade: an arc and the blast zone follow the cursor, click throws it (Shift+click keeps aiming; G / Esc / right-click cancels)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| E / left click / right click on a thing       | The cursor shows a small label over what the selected soldiers can use (**Pick** objects, **Drag** fallen allies and corpses, **Take** loose items, **Open** crates and doors, **Close**, **Locked**, **Powered**, **Switch on** / **Switch off**, **Man** (nest or tower), **Resupply** (turret or post), **Enter** vehicles). Click or E: the closest selected soldier walks there and does it (a vehicle takes all of them) |
| Ctrl+A                                        | Select all                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 1 – 9                                         | Select a unit by number (Shift adds)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Squad panel (bottom)                          | Click a unit to select it; shows each unit's health                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| V                                             | Next to a vehicle, nest or tower: climb in; inside, step out (the driver leaving takes the crew out too). **E** does the same                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Space / C / H (a helicopter's pilot selected) | **Space** starts the engine and takes off to 14 m, **C** lands (and shuts the engine down), **H** switches what it does when it has no destination: hover (default) or fly circles. A right-click move order takes off by itself, flies there and brakes on arrival                                                                                                                                                            |
| Y                                             | Take direct control of the first selected unit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Mouse wheel / right-drag                      | Zoom / rotate and tilt the camera                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

When you take control with **Y**, only the units that were selected follow you. The rest hold their position (or keep their move order). Every uncontrolled unit shoots the nearest enemy it can see.

| Mouse wheel                      | Zoom                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Right-drag on empty ground       | Rotate and tilt the camera                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Left click (tool armed)          | Place the armed unit or asset (arm it by clicking its entry in the Units / Assets tab); the Assets tab also lists Structures (built from the start), Objects and Loose items                                   |
| Right click / **Esc**            | Drop the armed tool                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Left click on an item            | Select it (a structure, object or loose item too; with one door or switch selected the panel shows its link, starts-open and the key it is locked with); **Shift**-click adds or removes it from the selection |

| Left-drag on empty ground        | Box select (Shift adds)                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Right-drag from an item          | Move that item                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

| **R** held + right-drag          | Turn the selection to face the pointer                                                                                                                                                                         |

## Mobile (landscape)

On a phone or tablet the game switches to a touch layout (add `?mobile=1` to the URL to try it on a desktop). On the deployed site only Map 1 is offered; add `?dev=1` to the URL to unlock every map (Start → Development). Hold the phone sideways. The small 👁 pad on the right turns the camera: drag it to look up and sideways (the first drag switches to third person), tap it to cycle normal, third and first person (the same cycle as the view button beside the action buttons, which reads ASYM, 3rd or 1st for the current view); in those views the squad fires at enemies near where you look. Grenades are not on the touch layout yet.

| Control               | Action                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Joystick (left)       | Drag a thumb anywhere in the lower-left area to move the whole squad; the others follow                                |
| Auto-fire             | The leader aims at the nearest enemy and fires on its own; so does the rest of the squad                               |
| Soldier icons         | Top-left row with each unit's health; tap one and direct control passes to it                                          |
| ⚙ / ☰ / 🔨 / 🛒 / 🎒 | Top-right: settings, main menu, build menu (maps with building on), shop (between waves), squad screen and inventories |
| Inventory             | Tap a weapon, then tap the slot to move it to (drag and drop also works where the browser allows)                      |
| 📦 (bottom-right)     | Appears when the leader stands next to a crate: tap to open it                                                         |
| 🧎 / 💨 / ⤒           | Crouch and sprint (tap to toggle) and jump (hold)                                                                      |
| Pinch / ⛶ / buzz      | Two-finger zoom; full screen button; the phone vibrates when a soldier is hurt                                         |

## Physics lab (Map 12)

Start → Single Player → Waves → Map 12 "Physics lab". The panel on the right drops things 10 m in front of the soldier: balls (100 or 1000), a pyramid, a marine-zombie ragdoll, joints-only ragdoll, a blast, five zombies and a blast. Sliders turn gravity, bounce, friction, contact stiffness, sub-steps, passes, bone damping and blast power; Pause, One step and Reset control the run; "Ver juntas y huesos" shows the joints. In first or third person the mouse is locked: press Esc to free it (the view returns to top-down). The four marines start with 8 frag grenades each here (Q throws, G aims in RTS): a grenade thrown into the pile pushes the bodies too.

## Ocean (Map 13)

Start → Single Player → Waves → Map 13 "Ocean test". A beach in the south, open sea with a sandbar and an island. The squad walks, swims (**C** dives, **Space** surfaces, **Shift** swims fast) and the Map 12 panel drops balls, boxes and ragdolls that float on the waves (they also ride the current). **,** and **.** turn the time of day. In the editor, the Environment tab holds the **Season** (spring to winter ground tint), the weather and the **Ocean water** group: the switch, the waves and current, buoyancy, **Whirlpools** (0 to 3, strength and width), **Water carries swimmers**, the **wave pattern** (golden, Fibonacci, random, parallel swell, custom angles; seed, chaos, swell direction and spread) and every optic of the water (light brake with depth, reddening, reflectivity, reflection, ripple shear, caustics, parallax, neon orbs, tint). **Sky and light** also holds the sun heading (turns the sun's path), the sun and moon halo, **Auto day** (hours per real minute), **Night light** (0 is pitch dark: only lamps and flashlights show) and the cloud thickness; **Weather** has rain, snow, **Snow on the ground**, sandstorm, fog (with a height falloff), **Wind** and **Lightning**, with Clear / Storm / Blizzard / Sandstorm / Fog buttons. In Settings the graphics list adds **Water**, **Sky clouds** and **Rain drops**.
