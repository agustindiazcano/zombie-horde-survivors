# The author's techniques (register and paper plan)

Who made what. The physics, fluid and optics techniques of this engine are the work of **Agustin Diaz Cano** (Wylan Studios), written first as single-file HTML demos in `game-engine/` (not in git) and then ported into the game. This file records them under the names he gave them, where each lives in the code, and which standard technique it sits next to. It is the base for the paper he plans to write.

**What this file does and does not claim.** Authorship is recorded as the author states it in his own demos (the labels "Diaz-Cano" in the HTML comments). Nobody has searched the literature or patents: whether a formula is new, a re-derivation or a re-parameterisation of something known is **not checked**. Many pieces are textbook parts (Beer-Lambert, Fresnel, Hooke contact, spatial hashing, fractal noise); the author's contribution is the particular formulas, constants and the way they are combined to be cheap and good-looking. The paper has to separate those honestly, with citations. Do not write "invented" or "novel" in public text before that check.

Rule for every session: keep the author's names for his techniques in docs and comments, never present them as standard, and name the standard technique next to them.

## 1. Register

| 2   | Mode `sapito`                                                                                                                                    | Second reflection curve `phi^(-(pi/2) / theta)` with a grazing factor (the label and the formula differ, noted in the 2026-10-23 review)                                                                                                                                                                | `oceanView.ts`                                                           | Same                                                                                    |
| 3   | Light brake by saturation ("freno por saturación")                                                                                               | Light left with depth `v = 1 - (S / 0.25)^phi`, red absorbed faster than blue by powers of phi                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `oceanView.ts`                                                           | Beer-Lambert law (`exp(-sigma d)`) with per-channel absorption                          |
| 2b  | Impedance `R0` ("impedancia de presión")                                                                                                         | `R0 = ((S_water - S_air) / (S_water + S_air))^2` with the author's saturation numbers. | 4   | Hooke crest ("cresta tensa de Hooke", `hooke`, `pressure`, `silk`)                                                                               | Wave shape `(1 - abs sin)^pressure` mixed with a plain sine; "silk" smooths the crest so the normal never breaks                                                                                                                                                                                        | `src/sim/ocean.ts`, `oceanView.ts` (PHYSICS.md 7)                        | Sum of sines, Gerstner waves, FFT ocean (Tessendorf)                                    |
| 5   | Golden-ratio wave scaling ("escalado fractal áureo"), with the patterns golden, Fibonacci, random, parallel swell and custom, and a `chaos` knob | Amplitude, frequency, speed and pressure of 8 waves scaled by phi-based ratios; directions on a golden-angle spiral                                                                                                                                                                                     | `src/sim/ocean.ts`                                                       | Wave spectra (Phillips, JONSWAP), fractal sums, swell with deep-water dispersion        |
| 6   | Archimedes + Hooke buoyancy ("Arquímedes + resorte de Hooke en el agua")                                                                         | A body under the surface gets lift proportional to sunk depth, a spring on the overlap, viscous drag and the current's pull                                                                                                                                                                             | `floatBodies` in `src/sim/physics/rapierWorld.ts`                        | Archimedes' principle, penalty-force buoyancy in game engines                           |
| 7   | Hooke O(1) collisions and the unified solver ("Física unificada")                                                                                | Spring plus damping contact on a spatial hash, constant cost per body, with sphere, box and ragdoll links in one solver                                                                                                                                                                                 | `src/sim/physics/world.ts` (own solver, kept as the Rust/WASM reference) | Penalty (spring-damper) contact, spatial hashing, position-based dynamics               |
| 8   | Inverse Archimedes and the "sub-Planckian sea" gravity                                                                                           | A thought model where gravity is a push from a fluid (used for the planet-gravity gauge of his Map 2 demo). Not used in the game                                                                                                                                                                        | `game-engine/` demos only                                                | None to compare in the engine; it is a theory, keep it apart from the engine techniques |
| 9   | "Law 3 of Diaz-Cano" and the golden whirlpools ("vórtices áureos")                                                                               | Whirlpool: a funnel `-20 s / (1 + (r/R)^1.65 + 0.25)`, a golden-spiral wave and a swirl with tangential speed `28 s / sqrt(r)` and an inward part scaled by `2 ln(phi) / pi * 1.35`. **Ported 2026-10-23** (bodies, swimmers and the water shader; the core geyser that throws a sunk body back up too) | `src/sim/ocean.ts` (`vortexHeight`, `oceanFlowAt`), `oceanView.ts`       | Rankine and Lamb-Oseen vortex models, Burgers vortex, logarithmic spirals               |
| 10  | Golden hash and golden fractal noise ("hash áureo", FBM áureo)                                                                                   | Noise whose octaves turn by the golden angle and scale by phi, used for stars, ripples, the volumetric clouds and the ice (clear and milky zones, bubbles)                                                                                                                                              | `environment.ts` (stars, clouds), `oceanView.ts` (`micro`, `iceFbm`)     | Fractal Brownian motion, value noise, Vogel's phyllotaxis (1979)                        |
| 11  | Golden-angle orbits ("órbita de Vogel", empaquetamiento áureo)                                                                                   | Neon orbs and other bodies spread by `radius ~ sqrt(n)` and the golden angle                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `oceanView.ts` (orbs)                                                    | Vogel's sunflower model, Fibonacci lattices                                             |
| 12  | The cheap-shader approach ("light and good-looking")                                                                                             | Analytic refraction, caustics and fog in one pass instead of render targets; the same wave function on CPU and GPU                                                                                                                                                                                      | `oceanView.ts`, `ocean.ts`                                               | Screen-space refraction, FFT ocean, Gerstner on the GPU                                 |

## 2. Paper plan

| Priority | Step                                                                                                                                               |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 1     | The author chooses the scope: one paper on the whole engine, or one on the water optics and one on the unified physics                             |
| 🟡 2     | Literature check for each row of the register: what is standard, what is a re-parameterisation, what looks new (needs web research and the author) |
| 🟡 3     | For each technique that survives: its formula in plain mathematics (taken from PHYSICS.md and GRAPHICS.md), its constants and why they were chosen |
| 🟡 4     | Measurements: cost per frame and per body against the standard technique (the repo already has `bench:sim`, `bench:physics`, `check:ocean`)        |
| 🟢 5     | Figures: screenshots of the same scene with each formula switched off and on (the graphics options already allow this)                             |
| ⚪ 6     | Venue, licence and what stays proprietary (the engine is proprietary, see LICENSE); Steam release does not depend on the paper                     |
