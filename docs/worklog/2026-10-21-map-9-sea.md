# 2026-10-21 — Map 9 "Sea test": carrier, frigate, island and speedboats

Branch `feat/helicopter` (no PR). Lint, format, typecheck, build, 48 tests pass. Nothing seen in a browser; a throw-away headless probe checked boarding from the deck, sailing, stopping at the hull and at the shore, stepping out on the beach and the frigate's hull (not kept in the repo).

- **Map:** 256 x 256 m (the biggest so far), water level 0, sea floor -3.5. `scripts/gen-map9.py` writes `data/maps/map-9.json` (800 kB of height grid and paint; excluded from Prettier in `.prettierignore`).
- **Carrier:** a `fixed` vehicle (`vehicles.carrier`, 150 x 34 m, bow 45 m, deck 3 m) drawn by `createCarrier` in `src/render/ships.ts`: grey hull over a red band, dark deck with centre line, angled landing line, three landing rings, catapult slots, a lift, five parked fighters and the island tower (a `block` prop collider keeps soldiers out of it). You walk on it because the **height grid has a raised plateau** under the hull (its rim is a 1 m cliff ring, so nobody falls off). Two helicopters (generic, Huey) stand on the rings; the four marines start next to them.
- **Frigate:** a `fixed` vehicle (62 x 9 m) at sea: bow gun, bridge, funnel, mast, aft hangar and helipad. Not walkable (no plateau); boats collide with its hull.
- **Island:** radius about 44 m with an irregular coast, a sand beach band, grass inland, hills, nine houses, trees and eight sleeping wanderers (the horde).
- **Speedboats:** `vehicles.speedboat` (`water: true`, 4 seats, door gun, enter range 5). `src/sim/naval.ts`: a boat floats only where the ground is at least 0.3 m below the water level at five hull points, is kept out of props and ship hulls (`shipHalfWidth`, the hull outline), and slides along the shore. Two lie alongside the carrier's port side, two off the island's west beach. A soldier can step out only onto dry, clear ground within 8 m (`landSpotNear`); a destroyed boat's crew swim to land up to 40 m away.
- **Schema (`vehicleType`):** `water`, `fixed`, `ship {length, width, bow, deck}`. Fixed vehicles are not interactable, not hurt, and skip `updateVehicles`. The hull outline formula is shared by the sim (`shipOutline`), the renderer and the Python generator.
- **Known limits:** the plateau is baked into the height grid, so moving the carrier in the editor does not move its deck; the carrier cannot be driven or entered; no wake, waves, sound or sinking; AI soldiers do not board boats by themselves; helicopters over the deck follow the 3 m plateau in the drawing but the sim has no deck height for vehicles; the map file is large. Tune: pad positions (`CARRIER_PAD_*` in ships.ts and gen-map9.py), boat numbers in `game.json`, `MIN_DEPTH` in naval.ts.
