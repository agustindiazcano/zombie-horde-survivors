# 2026-10-18 — Phase 1d: items and equipment

Branch `feat/items-equipment`, cut from `feat/unit-stats` (phases 1, 1b and 1c are not merged yet, so the PRs must go in order: `feat/map-format`, `feat/unit-stats`, `feat/items-equipment`). The game plays as before; the 48 tests and every static check pass.

- `data/items.json` + `ItemsSchema` (strict zod, a discriminated union on `type`) in `src/sim/config.ts`. Every item has `name`, `icon`, `stack`. Types: `weapon`, `armor` (chest), `plate`, `medical`, `grenade`, `defibrillator`, `accessory`. Fields under `mocked` are design data no rule reads; the squad screen marks them "not active yet".
- Weapon sheet: `class`, `hands` (1 or 2), `damageType` (`physical`, `explosive`, `fire`, and the experimental `energy`, `psychic`, `magic`, `scifi`; only `physical` exists in play), `fireRate`, `damage`, `range`, `spread`, plus `droneOnly` and `innate`. Mocked: `magazine`, `reloadTime`, `noise`, `weight`, `accessorySlots`.
- Catalog: fists (innate, melee), pistol, rifle, heavy MG, drone gun, armor plate, kevlar vest, medkits S / M / L (heal 25 / 50 / 100), frag grenade, defibrillator, and three accessories (scope, suppressor, foregrip). `game.json` no longer has `weapons`, `weapon` or the drone's fire stats: weapons live only in `items.json`. `drops.table[].weapon` became `item`; shop items of kind `weapon` became `item`. `parseConfig(raw, map, units, items)` checks that `squad.startWeapon`, shop targets and crate entries are real items.
- Slots: `Soldier.equipment` = `rightHand`, `leftHand`, `chest` (replaces `weapon`); the backpack is 10 slots (`inventory.backpackSlots`, was 6) of stacks `{ item, count }`. A two-handed weapon only fits `rightHand` and needs `leftHand` empty; a drone only takes drone guns in `rightHand`; hands take weapons only; the chest takes armor. With both hands empty the unit has fists (shown, not active: it does not fight).
- `src/sim/inventory.ts`: `moveItem` works on typed slots (swap, move, merge stacks, rolls back if a rule breaks), `equipFromBackpack` (stows the off-hand for a two-hander), `unequipSlot` (replaces `unequipWeapon`), `useItem` (a medkit heals; the rest have no rule), `addToBackpack`, crate take / stash with stacks. A unit shoots with its right-hand gun, else the left (`gunOf` in `stats.ts`); dual wield does not fire both hands yet.
- UI: the squad screen unit window has an equipment column (right hand, left hand, vest; "Fists" caption when empty) beside the 10-slot backpack grid, with Equip / Unequip / Use buttons and a tooltip per item (`src/itemInfo.ts`). The crate window (`inventoryUi.ts`) uses the same item labels.
- Tests: the 48 are unchanged in number. `tests/sim/helpers.ts` builds the item catalog (its `weapon` patch now overrides the rifle item), and `config.test.ts` damages `zombie` instead of the removed `weapon` section.
- Not reachable in play yet on purpose (the game must play as before): medkits, grenades, the defibrillator, the vest and the accessories are in the catalog but not in the drop table or the shop.
- Not done: helmet slot (the plan lists it; this phase was asked for hands and vest only), weapon meshes on the models (asked to the user before the visual part), dual-wield firing, melee and fists combat, throwing, ammo and reloading.
