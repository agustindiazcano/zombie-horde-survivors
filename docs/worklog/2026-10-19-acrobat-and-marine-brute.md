# 2026-10-19 — Acrobat marine and a marine brute

Same branch as W1 to W5 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). 48 tests and all checks pass; **nothing seen in a browser**. First steps of [the marine variants plan](../PLAN-marine-zombies-and-dismemberment.md), done early so the user can try them.

- **`marine_acrobat`:** the marine model tinted violet (`#9b6bd6`), `Run_03` as its run clip, speed x1.9, health x0.7, damage x0.8, weight 4 from wave 1 (it shows up in normal waves; also in the editor's Units > Enemies and as a spawner). Its `acrobat` block in `data/game.json` (`range` 12 m, `interval` 1.1 s, `duration` 0.55 s, `sideSpeed` 5 m/s, `height` 1.3 m): while it runs at its target and is between 2 m beyond its reach and `range` away, it hops every so often, to the left, to the right or straight up (one third each). The sim keeps `Zombie.y`, `hopLeft`, `hopSide`, `hopTimer` (`hop()` in `src/sim/zombies.ts`); the renderer lifts the model by `y` and turns it once over during the hop (a cartwheel to the side, a somersault when straight up). Checked headless with a throwaway test (it hops over 1 m high on the way in), not committed.
- **Marine brute:** `brute` now uses the marine model at scale 1.4 with a burgundy tint (`#c0605a`), `Slow_Orc_Walk`, `Attack`, `Dead`; its stats and collision (`radiusScale` 1.5) did not change. The old Quaternius brute model stays in `public/models`.
- **Not done / to check:** the acrobat is still hit by every bullet while airborne (a dodge rule would be one line in `fireWeapon`); the marine has 61k triangles, so many acrobats and brutes at once may cost frames (measure with F3; see section 9 of the plan); the look of the violet and burgundy tints, the size of the brute and the spin of the hop are for the user to judge.
