# 2026-10-19 — Weapons W3: accuracy, recoil and aiming

Same branch as W1 and W2 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). 48 tests and all checks pass; nothing seen in a browser.

- **Accuracy (`src/sim/accuracy.ts`):** the random aim error of a shot is the weapon's `spread` (now its worst case, at skill level 1) times a movement factor (`1 + movementSpread x speed/walking speed`, up to 2x speed), a weapon-class skill factor (1 down to `skillBestScale` at `skillMaxLevel`), a stand-still factor, and the aiming factor, never below `minError`, plus the accumulated recoil. Crouch and the downed penalty were already in the weapon stats. All numbers are in the new `accuracy` section of `game.json`. The AI uses the same rule, so it misses more than before.
- **Rebalance of base spreads:** pistol 0.02 to 0.04, rifle 0 to 0.03, heavy MG 0.07 to 0.08, drone gun still exact.
- **Recoil:** a `recoil` block on pistol, rifle and heavy MG in `data/items.json` (`kick`, `maxKick`, `recovery`, `sideBias`, `cameraKick`); a gun without the block has none. Each shot adds `kick` (x0.5 aiming, x0.7 crouching, `Soldier.recoil`); it fades by `recovery` radians per second, widens the error of the next shots and drifts them to one side by `sideBias` (the side depends on the soldier's id). The `shot` event carries `cameraKick`, added to the local player's camera shake.
- **Aim (hold the right mouse button):** third and first person only (`Input.aiming`; main sets it from the right button, or from a click-to-toggle if the new Settings option "Aim: click to toggle" is on). While aiming: spread x0.3, recoil x0.5, walking speed x0.5, no sprint, and the view zooms to 0.75 of the field of view with an ease. A hit sets `aimBroken` until the button is released; going down, sprinting, entering a vehicle or changing view (T) also cancel it. Right-drag camera rotation already did nothing in the person views.
- **Dynamic crosshair:** its size follows the current aim error (`aimErrorOf`): 12 px at no error, 700 px per radian, capped at 120.
- **Top-down and RTS:** no aim button; standing still for 0.5 s gives x0.8 spread (the AI too).
- **Tests:** the shared test config gives the rifle no spread and no recoil so the geometry tests stay exact.
- Checked headless with throwaway tests (still < moving, aiming < standing, aiming halves speed, recoil builds while firing and fades, a hit breaks the aim until release), not committed.
- **Not done:** third-person camera moving closer to the shoulder; weapon model lift; a bigger scope zoom (W6); skill XP is still earned only from kills; no stand-still hint in the HUD; every number is an estimate and the squad's survival at wave 3+ will probably change.
