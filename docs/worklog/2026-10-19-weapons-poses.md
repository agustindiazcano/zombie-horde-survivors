# 2026-10-19 — Weapon poses: two-handed aim and the first-person gun

Same branch as W1 to W5 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). 48 tests and all checks pass; **nothing seen in a browser, and every position is a guess** for the Meshy marine.

- **Third person and the AI (`src/render/armPose.ts`):** after the animation runs, two-bone inverse kinematics puts the right hand on the grip in front of the shoulder with the barrel along the heading, and, for a long gun (rifle, heavy MG: `foregrip` in `WEAPON_POSES`), the left hand on the foregrip wherever the weapon ended up. The result is blended over the clip's pose by `CharacterView.aim(amount)`. The renderer raises the arms while a soldier aims (`Soldier.aiming`) and for 0.8 s after each shot, then drops them back to the clip; a melee strike (F) cancels it. Pistol: one arm extended; knife: untouched. Constants to tune: `RIGHT_HAND_TWO_HANDED`, `RIGHT_HAND_ONE_HANDED`, the elbow hints in `armPose.ts`, and the foregrip offsets in `weaponModels.ts`.
- **First person (`src/render/viewModel.ts`):** the controlled soldier's gun is drawn in its own pass on top of the world with a fixed 62° camera (no clipping into walls, no size change when the view zooms). It sits at the hip, rises to the sights when aiming (the iron-sight line meets the screen centre), kicks back and up on each shot, dips and tilts while reloading, and sways when walking. Hands and sleeves are boxes. Positions per weapon (`hip`, `ads`) are in `WEAPON_POSES` in `weaponModels.ts`.
- **Not done:** the aim pitch is not applied to the third-person pose; no muzzle flash from the barrel; the left hand has no finger poses; a low-ready pose when idle; the squad screen and menu previews still show no weapons.
