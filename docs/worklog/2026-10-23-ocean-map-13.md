| Part                      | What                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `src/sim/ocean.ts`        | `oceanHeight(params, level, groundY, x, z, t)`, `oceanFlow`, the eight wave directions                                                                                              |
| `game.json` `ocean`       | Defaults of the waves, current and buoyancy; a map overrides any in `logic.ocean` (its presence is the `ocean` rule, `config.oceanOn`)                                              |
| `rapierWorld.ts`          | `ocean` and `oceanTime`; `floatBodies` before each step (lift, vertical damping, pull to the current)                                                                               |
| `src/render/oceanView.ts` | The water plane: vertex waves identical to the sim, fragment with depth colour, golden-ratio Fresnel (two curves), caustics, parallax ripples, sun and moon glints, foam, neon orbs |
| `environment.water`       | The look per map: fresnel, light brake, red absorption, reflectivity, reflection, ripple shear, caustics, parallax steps and depth, neon orbs, tint                                 |
| `environment.season`      | Spring, summer, autumn, winter: tints the ground (winter adds a snow glow)                                                                                                          |
| Editor                    | Environment tab: Season buttons and the Ocean water group (switch, waves, current, buoyancy, every look slider, tint)                                                               |
| Map 13                    | `scripts/gen-map13.py`: 160 m, beach, sandbar, island; rules `ocean`, `swim`, `physicsLab`, `timeControl`; dusk with 4 neon orbs                                                    |
