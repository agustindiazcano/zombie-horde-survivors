# 2026-10-19 — Marine M1: variants and procedural blood

Same branch as W1 to W6 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). All checks pass; **nothing seen in a browser**.

- **Old types now wear the marine:** `skeleton` is the brown **Rotten marine** (`Walking`), `alien` the **Gory marine** (`Run_03`, `Triple_Combo_Attack`, blood-soaked), `brute` the **Burgundy marine** (more blood). Ids, unlock waves, weights and multipliers did not change, so the difficulty curve is the same and bosses (which cycle these ids) wear the marine too. The old models stay in `public/models`, the hound stays until the crawler (M4).
- **New `marine_bleeder`:** green, health x2.5, from wave 4, weight 2, reward x2; every ~1.2 s a short jet of blood leaves its torso (`Gore.spurt`, respects the gore graphics option).
- **Procedural blood (`src/render/bloodTexture.ts`):** `blood` (0 to 1) in `data/characters.json` paints stains, splatter and drips over a copy of the model's colour map with a canvas, seeded so it is the same every load; no image asset. `spurts` sets the seconds between jets.
- **To tune after seeing it:** the tint colours, `blood` amounts (stains land on random UV areas, so some may fall on empty texture), spurt rate and height, the bleeder's stats.
- **Not done:** wet shine, the bleeder's trail on the ground, bosses with armour plates (M5), the crawler (M4).
