# 2026-10-19 — Grenades

Branch `feat/grenades` (cut from `main` after the stack merged). 48 tests and all checks pass; nothing seen in a browser.

- **Rules:** `frag_grenade` is now real (`blastRadius`, `blastDamage`, `falloff`, `fuse`, `throwRange` on the item in `data/items.json`; only `weight` stays mocked). New `grenades` section in `game.json` (`throwSpeed` 14, `releaseHeight` 1.4, `gravity` 20, `cooldown` 0.8, `friendlyFire` 0.5). Obtained from crates (weight 1.5 in the drop table) and the shop (30 gold, into the selected unit's backpack).
- **Sim:** `src/sim/grenades.ts`. `throwGrenade` takes one from the backpack and lobs it along a parabola (closed form, flight = distance / throwSpeed); a target past `throwRange` is pulled in (`grenadeAim`). It rests where it lands and explodes when the fuse ends (never before landing). The blast hurts enemies (kill credit to the thrower), allies at `friendlyFire` of the damage, and occupied vehicles; damage falls off linearly with distance. The throw sets the thrower's `fireCooldown`. Events: `grenadeThrown`, `explosion`.
- **Direct control:** **Q** throws at the aim point (cursor on the ground in top-down, the view point in third / first person).
- **RTS:** **G** (with a unit selected that carries a grenade) shows the arc and the blast zone following the cursor (orange; yellow when the cursor is past the range), a left click throws it from the selected unit closest to the target, Shift+click keeps aiming, G / Esc / right-click cancels. Without a grenade in the selection the hint line says so. In direct control G is still dragging a body.
- **Render:** `src/render/grenades.ts` (ball in flight, fireball with a light, preview arc and zone); a small screen shake and procedural sounds on throw and blast.
- Checked headless with a throwaway test (throw, cooldown, range clamp, blast kills, thrower unharmed), not committed.
- **Not done:** AI soldiers never throw; no cooking or bounce; the arc flies over walls and the blast ignores cover; other damage types (fire, smoke); throwing from a vehicle; a mobile button.
