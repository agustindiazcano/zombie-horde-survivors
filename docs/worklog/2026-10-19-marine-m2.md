# 2026-10-19 — Marine M2: the head comes off

Same branch as W1 to W6, the flow field and M1b (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). Lint, format, typecheck, the 48 tests, build and the asset check pass; **nothing seen in a browser**.

- **Rule (sim):** a head hit that **kills** sets `zombieKilled.head` to `'burst'` when the weapon's base damage is at least `dismember.headBurstDamage` (25: rifle and drone cannon burst, pistol and machine gun knock it off) and to `'fly'` otherwise. A head hit that does not kill only does the W4 extra damage. An enemy that bursts whole (`explodeChance`) has no head event. `damageZombie` got a `force` argument (the weapon's base damage; `weapon.ts` passes it).
- **Mesh split (`src/render/headSplit.ts`):** `splitHead` runs once per loaded model flagged `"dismember": true` in `data/characters.json` (all the light-marine enemies). Each vertex counts as head when more than half of its bone weight is on `Head` or a bone below it (head top, `headfront`); a triangle is head when two of its three vertices are. The index is reordered body first, head last, and two geometry groups (two materials) are made. Checked on `soldier-marine-light.glb` with a script: 436 of 4,900 triangles are head. Cost: two draw calls per marine instead of one.
- **View:** `CharacterView.removeHead({ loose, stump })` hides the head material, puts a dark-red sphere (`STUMP_RADIUS` 0.09 m, scaled to undo the rig's scale) on the head bone and, for a flying head, returns a frozen copy of the model drawing only the head, centred on the head bone. `restoreHead()` runs when a view returns to the pool.
- **Renderer:** `loseHead` (on `zombieKilled`): a burst calls `Gore.headBurst` (droplets and chunks, no puddle); a flying head is thrown away from the shooter by `LooseParts` (`src/render/looseParts.ts`: gravity, bounces, tumble, 6 s, fade, at most 12). The headless body sprays from the neck for 1.2 s (`NECK_SPURT_TIME`) while it falls. The `gore` graphics option off: the head just disappears, with no stump, blood or loose head. The burst plays the splat sound.
- **Not done:** only the killing hit takes the head (by decision); bosses and the full-size squad marine have no head split (squad enemies only); the loose head is posed once and does not keep the death animation; no sound for a flying head; `headBurstDamage` and the `STUMP_RADIUS` / throw speeds are guesses.
