# 2026-10-20 — Door system v2 (W1) and large gates (W2)

Branch `feat/doors-v2` (from `feat/soldier-structure-pathing`, with `feat/corpses-and-loose-items` merged in because the keycard lies on the ground as a loose item).

## What a door can do now

All data, in `structures.types.*.doorRules` of `data/game.json` (defaults in `DEFAULT_DOOR_RULES`, `src/sim/config.ts`):

| Field                             | Meaning                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `look`                            | `swing` (hinge at one end, turns 90 degrees), `slide` (one leaf along the wall), `double` (two leaves part), `shutter` (rolls up)         |
| `openTime`                        | Seconds from shut to wide open. The door blocks until 85% open (`PASS_OPENNESS`) and as soon as it starts to shut                         |
| `auto`, `autoRange`, `closeDelay` | Opens for a soldier on its feet (or a driven vehicle) within `autoRange` of its footprint, shuts `closeDelay` s after the last one leaves |
| `seeThrough`                      | Glass: sight (wanderers, `canSee`) passes when shut; bullets and bodies do not                                                            |
| `powered`                         | E does nothing (`doorDenied: powered`); it follows any built `switch` of the same `link` that is on                                       |

Per structure (`Structure.openness`, `lock`, `link`, `idle`): a map's `logic.structures` entries take `open`, `lock` (a `key` item id) and `link`. A locked door opens when a soldier carrying that key uses it (**E**, a click in RTS, the build panel button) and stays unlocked afterwards. A door will not shut on someone standing in it (`doorDenied: blocked`); the HUD notice says why a door refused.

New: item type `key` (`keycard_red`, `keycard_blue`), structure flag `switch` (E toggles it, a small lamp shows red / green), `logic.items` in maps (loose items on the ground from the start, e.g. a keycard).

## Door types added (build bar and Map 7)

Glass door, sliding door (auto), large gate (5.2 m, double, 1.6 s, a jeep fits), garage shutter, sci-fi door (double, auto, 0.6 s), blast door (powered, slides in 3 s, 2500 health) and the wall switch. Status lamps on locked and powered doors: red locked, amber unpowered, green on.

**Map 7 "Door gallery"** (`scripts/gen-map7.py`): a wall with one of each door in a row, a keycard on the ground in front of the locked door, a switch for the blast door, build mode on with 600 gold, and six sleeping marines on the far side.

## Soldiers and the AI

Doors the AI may use (not locked, not powered, not auto) are open ground in the ally grid and are opened by `openDoorAhead`; locked and powered doors count as walls for the AI's path. Auto doors open by themselves as the soldier arrives. Zombies still bite any shut door in the way.

## Limits

- The AI never closes doors and never uses a key or a switch on its own; a locked or powered door is a wall for its path.
- A swing door always turns towards the +perpendicular side of its footprint (one hinge end, no choice yet).
- Build mode can place the new doors and the switch (link 0, so they pair with each other), but locks and links are set only in a map file; the map editor does not place structures yet.
- Sliding leaves go into the neighbouring wall without a recess; airlock pairs (W3) are not built.
