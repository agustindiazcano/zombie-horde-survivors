# 2026-10-19 — Weapons W5: melee

Same branch as W1 to W3 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). 48 tests and all checks pass; nothing seen in a browser.

- **Rules (`src/sim/melee.ts`):** `meleeStrike` hits the nearest living enemy whose edge is within reach and inside a cone in front (`melee.arc`); an enemy touching the unit is always in reach. The blow is a knife if one is in a hand (damage and range from the item), else the gun's butt (`melee.bashDamage`, `bashRange`), else the fists of the catalog. Damage scales with the damage perk. The enemy is shoved `melee.push` metres divided by its weight (radius over base radius; bosses keep `bossPushScale` of it) and staggered `melee.stagger` s (`Zombie.stagger`: no moving, no biting). The cooldown is shared with firing (`melee.cooldown`), the strike interrupts a reload; downed units, drones and units in vehicles cannot.
- **Input:** **F** in direct control (`main.ts`); F stays "follow" in RTS.
- **AI:** a soldier with an enemy within `melee.aiRange` of its edge turns to it and strikes instead of shooting, then goes back to shooting when the enemy is pushed away; an unarmed soldier now punches.
- **Data:** new `knife` item (melee class, 35 damage, range 1.4; crate drop weight 1.5, not in the shop) and a `melee` section in `game.json`.
- **Render and sound:** the `melee` event plays the marine's `Attack` clip for 0.5 s (new `attack` clip in `data/characters.json`), flashes the enemy and shakes the camera a little; a short procedural thud.
- Checked headless with a throwaway test (hit, shove, stagger, shared cooldown, AI strike), not committed.
- **Not done:** a knife slash clip (the marine only has the punch / attack ones), a combo, melee skill XP separate from the gun's, crits (W4), mobile button, no push of allies.
