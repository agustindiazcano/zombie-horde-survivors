# 2026-10-19 — Weapons W2: magazines, ammo and reload

Same branch as W1 (`feat/weapon-instances`; W1 is not merged yet). 48 tests and all checks pass; nothing seen in a browser.

- **Data:** new item type `ammo` (`pack` = rounds per crate or shop unit; a backpack stack counts rounds): `ammo_556` (stack 120, pack 30) and `ammo_belt` (stack 300, pack 100). Weapons gain `ammo` (which item they load) and `infiniteSpare` (the pistol: spare magazines never run out, as decided). `ammo.dryFireDelay` in `game.json`. The schema checks that `ammo` points to an ammo item and that a magazine has a source of rounds.
- **Rules (`src/sim/ammo.ts`):** a shot spends one loaded round; at zero the trigger clicks (`dryFire` event, delay `dryFireDelay`). **R** (`startReload`) loads from the backpack's stacks for the reload time; no fire while reloading; the reload is dropped if the unit goes down or dies or the hand holds another weapon (the reload remembers the weapon state it started on). The drone gun, fists and any weapon without a magazine never run dry.
- **AI (and touch play):** `maintainAmmo` reloads an empty gun at once and tops up a part-empty one when there is nothing to shoot at; it runs for AI soldiers and passengers. Touch play has no R, so the controlled unit reloads itself.
- **Sources:** shop (Rifle ammo 15 gold, Machine gun belt 40) and crates (weights 2.5 and 1); a new rifle or machine gun arrives with a full magazine and no spare ammo. Crates now keep weapon state (`Crate.states`), so stashing and taking a gun back does not refill it; ammo stashed in a crate goes back as whole packs (rounded up).
- **UI:** the HUD health line shows `rounds/magazine + spare` (or ∞) and "reloading"; the squad screen shows the loaded rounds on each gun; tooltip for ammo; sounds for the empty click and the reload start.
- **Docs:** CONTROLS.md (R), README, the direct-control hint line.
- Checked headless with throwaway tests (30 shots then dry fire, no fire while reloading, the backpack stack shrinks by the loaded rounds, the infinite pistol reloads, a rifle without ammo cannot, the AI reloads, crate round trip), not committed.
- **Not done:** an unarmed-weapon swap key; a manual "interrupt" reload; reload animation; ammo for turrets (build plan); the starting squad has no spare rifle ammo; picking ammo up from the ground (only crates and shop); maps cannot yet set `loaded` on a placed weapon.
