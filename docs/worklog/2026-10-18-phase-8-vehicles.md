# 2026-10-18 — Phase 8: vehicles

Branch `feat/vehicles`, cut from `feat/game-logic` (tenth in the stack; PRs go in order). 48 tests and all checks pass; nothing seen in a browser. Decisions taken from the plan's recommendations: arcade steering, land vehicles only.

- **Data:** `vehicles` section in `data/game.json` (schema `vehicleType` in `config.ts`): health, maxSpeed, accel, turnRate, radius, seats, passengersShoot, enterRange, ramDps, ramSpeed. One type so far: `jeep` (4 seats, mounted gun, so passengers shoot). Catalog: Units > Vehicles > Military > Land > Jeep.
- **Sim (`src/sim/vehicles.ts`):** `Vehicle` in `state.vehicles` (seat 0 = driver; no driver, no movement). `Soldier.vehicleId` marks who is inside. `toggleVehicle` climbs into the closest vehicle with a free seat or steps out (the driver leaving takes the whole crew out). A controlled soldier taking the wheel brings the following soldiers within 12 m. Arcade driving: the hull turns towards the direction asked (WASD, camera relative) and accelerates in proportion to how well it faces it; an AI driver follows a move order (RTS right-click); obstacles and water block it (a crash keeps 40% of the speed). Above `ramSpeed` it runs enemies over (`ramDps`, kills credit the driver).
- **Combat:** zombies go for the nearest soldier on foot or an _occupied_ vehicle (they stop at the hull); soldiers inside cannot be hit, only the vehicle takes damage; at zero health it is destroyed and the crew get out. Passengers shoot only when `passengersShoot` (the mouse aims for the controlled one, the AI picks the nearest enemy); the driver only drives. Empty vehicles are not targeted.
- **Map / editor:** a placed unit whose type is in `vehicles` becomes a vehicle in `createGame`; the editor draws it like any other unit (it comes from the game state).
- **Render:** `src/render/vehicles.ts` builds the jeep from boxes and cylinders (no model yet, no new asset); it follows the terrain, turns smoothly, wheels spin. Soldiers inside are hidden. `V` shows a floating bubble near a vehicle (`CratePrompt` now takes the key letter); the hint line shows the jeep's health while riding.
- **Controls:** `V` enter / leave (RTS: the selected soldiers). README updated.
- Not done: RTS "enter this vehicle" orders and AI soldiers that board on their own, wreck left behind when destroyed, no sound or explosion effect, no health bar over the vehicle, vehicles do not tilt on slopes, a proper model, air / sea vehicles, crew injury, mobile button.
