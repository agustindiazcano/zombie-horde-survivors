# 2026-10-19 — Marine M1b: wanderers and climbers

Same branch as W1 to W6 and the flow field (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). All checks pass, the 48 tests included; **nothing seen in a browser**.

- **Wanderer (`marine_wanderer`, `src/sim/wander.ts`):** pale marine, `Zombie.mode` `wander` until it notices the squad, then `chase` for good at its run speed (x1.7). It spawns somewhere in the arena (not at the edge), out of the squad's sense range, and strolls to random points within `roamRadius` of its home (`Casual_Walk`), stopping 1 to 4 s between walks (`Alert` clip as the idle). It wakes when a soldier is within `senseRadius` (5 m), or within `sightRadius` (16 m) inside its `sightArc` cone with a clear line (`canSee`), or when a neighbour wakes: the shout chains through every sleeper within `shoutRadius` (10 m). A `zombieAlerted` event plays a growl. A map can place one with the editor's spawner / Units > Enemies and it sleeps where it stands.
- **Noise:** `hearShot` wakes sleepers within `noise x noiseMetres` of a shot, but `wander.noiseAttracts` in `game.json` is **false** (decision: off until balanced). `WeaponDef.noise` now carries the suppressor's effect.
- **No stuck waves:** a wave only ends when all enemies are dead, so sleepers wake `wander.wakeDelay` (25 s) after the wave has finished spawning.
- **Climber (`marine_climber`):** orange marine, `Running` clip, walks straight at its target over any obstacle up to `climber.maxHeight` (2.2 m, every box is 2 m; water and cliffs still stop it) and rises onto the box under it and drops off the far side (`Zombie.y`, procedural, no clip). The straight-line check uses a second flow-field grid with only the obstacles it cannot cross (`canSee`, `steerTarget(..., climbHeight)`); when a wall it cannot cross is in the way it follows the same field as walkers.
- **Data:** new `wander` section in `game.json` and the test helper; `wander` and `climber` blocks per enemy; `roam` and `idle` clips in `characters.json` (the animation controller falls back to the walk clip); both variants in `data/catalog.json`.
- Checked headless with a throwaway test (sleeps near home, wakes near, shouts only within range, a climber gets over a box), not committed.
- **Not done:** the exclamation icon above an alerted head; vehicles do not wake wanderers; crouching does not shrink the sense radius; bosses neither wander nor climb (the plan says bosses climb); the flow field has no climb cost, so a climber facing an unclimbable wall detours like a walker; wanderer and climber weights, radii and the 25 s wake delay are guesses.
