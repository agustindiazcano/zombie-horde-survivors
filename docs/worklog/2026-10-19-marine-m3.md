# 2026-10-19 — Marine M3: body zones and limbs

Same branch as W1 to W6, the flow field, M1b and M2 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). Lint, format, typecheck, the 48 tests, build and the asset check pass; **nothing seen in a browser**.

- **Zones (`src/sim/hits.ts`):** `rollHit` now returns a `zone` (`head`, `torso`, `arm`, `leg`) and an arm `side`. With a hit height (third / first person): below `dismember.legBand` (45%) of the enemy's height is a leg; above it a `armChance` (15%) takes an arm, else the torso. Without a height (top-down, the AI, melee): `legChance` (20%) leg, then `armChance` arm, else torso. Only enemies with `"limbs": true` in `game.json` (the ten marine variants) have zones.
- **Wounds (`src/sim/dismember.ts`):** hits on a limb add up in `Zombie.wounds`; at `legFraction` (25%) / `armFraction` (20%) of max health the limb comes off (mini bosses need x`bossResist` 3). A killing hit on a limb also takes it off with `killSeverChance` (50%). Grenades: each limb of an enemy in the blast comes off with `blastSeverChance` (35%, less at the edge). A `dismember` event reports it (limb, position, throw heading).
- **Effects:** one arm lost: bite x0.6; both: x0.35 in all. Legs lost: speed x`crawlSpeed` (0.35), no climbing, and its hit height shrinks to `crawlHeight` (45%) so head shots need a lower aim. The head rule of M2 is unchanged.
- **Mesh (`src/render/limbSplit.ts`, replaces `headSplit.ts`):** the light marine is split at load into body, head, left arm, right arm and legs (triangles counted on the file: 728 / 436 / 744 / 720 / 2,272). The parts lie in order in the index, so an intact marine still draws in **one** call; only a marine that lost something gets its own geometry (shared vertices) with a group per run of what is left. Each cut gets a red stump (head 9 cm, arm 7 cm, leg 10 cm radius); the loose limb is a frozen copy drawing only that range, thrown by `LooseParts` (rests at `LIMB_REST` 0.2 m / `LEG_REST` 0.35 m).
- **Legless look (a first version of M4):** the body is pitched forward `CRAWL_PITCH` (1.25 rad) and shifted back `CRAWL_BACK` (0.5 m) while it keeps its walk clip. The real crawl, the `marine_crawler` enemy and the cut-in-two kill are M4.
- **Not done:** bosses lose limbs only by the same rule (the enraged boss is M5); arms and legs are not told apart by side for the legs; loose limbs do not leave blood trails; `gore` off: limbs just vanish; every number is a guess. Checked headless with a throwaway test (legs off after two hits, speed and bite scaled), not committed.
