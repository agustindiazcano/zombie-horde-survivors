# 2026-10-21 — Toaster preset, more surfaces, glass, sirens, a bigger Map 11

Branch `feat/helicopter` (not pushed, no PR). Lint, format, typecheck, build, check:assets and the 48 tests pass. The shaders and shapes were checked by rendering throw-away pages in headless Chrome (walls, floors, glass, siren); the full game and Map 11 itself were not opened.

- **Toaster preset** (`src/settings.ts`): `resolutionScale` 0.35 (the slider now goes down to 25%), no shadows, no gore, no parallax; below 50% the canvas gets `image-rendering: pixelated` so it is drawn in blocks. A new `parallax` setting (0 off, 1 light, 2 full) with a "Surface depth" select in the settings panel; presets: toaster 0, low 1, medium 1, high 2. `applyGraphics` calls `setPomQuality`.
- **Surfaces** (`src/render/pomPatterns.ts`, procedural, nothing downloaded): walls brick, stone, plates, concrete, wood, corrugated, sci-fi wall, alien wall; floors cobble, metal (diamond plate), sci-fi, alien, dirt, sand, wet (puddles are flat, dark and mirror-like), road (dashed yellow line, white edge lines, cracks). Each has its own carving depth and tile size (2 to 4 m). Glowing lines and veins use an emissive map and some use a roughness map; both are read at the parallax-shifted point.
- **Speed:** a lookup-table noise and `sqrt` instead of `Math.hypot`; 256 px textures; the patterns are evaluated a few milliseconds per frame (`work()` in `pom.ts`), so a map never freezes: the surfaces start flat grey and fill in over a few seconds. All 16 together cost about 1.2 s of CPU.
- **Glass** (`src/render/glass.ts`): `glass_clear`, `blue`, `green`, `smoke`, `frosted`, `cracked` in a metal frame with a mullion. The clear, blue, green and cracked panes are see-through for the line of sight (`seeThrough`); smoke and frosted are not. Bodies and bullets never cross.
- **Sirens** (`src/render/sirens.ts`): `siren` (a pole) and `police_car` (the car prop, white with a dark band) carry a light bar with a red and a blue half that flash twice in turn, each with a glow sprite and a real point light (intensity 40, range 26). Lights exist only on a map that has sirens.
- **Asset names and heights:** `src/sim/lab.ts` (`pom_<kind>`, `glass_<kind>`, `siren`, `police_car`).
- **Map 11** is now 160 x 160 (`scripts/gen-map11.py`): a row of eight floor slabs, a long road where the squad starts, two rows of the eight walls, a glass gallery in front of a dark wall, a police scene (patrol car, siren post, wet street, walls) in the west, a sci-fi and alien corner in the east, pillars. It starts at 19:00 so the lights and glows show; **,** and **.** move the clock.
- **Not done / guesses:** the sun shadow darkens ambient light too; the wet floor has no real reflections (no environment map), only the sun and siren glints; glass has no reflections; no sound for sirens; the ground of the terrain is still flat colour; the light bar on the patrol car is a plain box.
