# 2026-10-19 — Weapons W6: accessories and the workbench

Same branch as W1 to W5 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). All checks pass; **nothing seen in a browser**.

- **Data:** accessories now have real `modifiers` (schema `accessoryMods` in `src/sim/config.ts`: `damage`, `range`, `spread`, `aimSpread`, `recoil`, `noise`, `reloadTime`, `magazine`, `zoom`; fractions added to the weapon's value, `magazine` in rounds). Eight items in `data/items.json`: red dot, scope (optic), suppressor, compensator (barrel), extended and quick magazine (magazine), foregrip (grip), laser (underbarrel). Only the weight stays `mocked`. They are in the crate table and five of them in the shop (40 to 70 gold).
- **Rules:** `src/sim/accessoryMods.ts` sums the fitted modifiers (`modsOf`, `factor`, never below 10% of a stat). `weaponOf` applies damage, range and spread; `spreadNow` the extra aiming spread; `kickRecoil` the recoil; `ammo.ts` the magazine size and reload time (`magazineOf`); `aimZoomOf` narrows the view while aiming. `fitAccessory` and `removeAccessory` (`src/sim/accessories.ts`) move an accessory between the backpack and a weapon (in a hand or in the backpack): a taken place swaps, a magazine that gets smaller trims the loaded rounds.
- **Workbench (`src/workbench.ts`):** the **⚙** on a weapon in the squad screen (so only while paused) opens its window: the weapon's places, the accessories in the backpack (hover to preview), and a table Bare / Now / With with the numbers coloured better or worse. Esc or a click outside closes it.
- **Noise:** the number is shown and a suppressor lowers it, but no enemy hears shots: every enemy chases from spawn today. The attract-by-noise rule belongs to the wanderers of M1b (decision 6 of the weapons plan: off by default).
- Checked headless with a throwaway test (fit, swap, remove, magazine trim, a slot a weapon lacks), not committed.
- **Not done:** accessories do not show on the weapon models; a map cannot place a weapon with accessories fitted (a loadout can carry loose accessories); the `stock` place has no weapon or item; no sound or look change for a suppressor; the numbers are estimates.
