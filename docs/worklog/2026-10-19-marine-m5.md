# 2026-10-19 — Marine M5: bosses as marines

Same branch as M4 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). Lint, format, typecheck, the 48 tests, build and the asset check pass; **nothing seen in a browser**.

- **Types:** `boss.types` is now zombie, skeleton, alien, brute, `marine_bleeder` (the first four are already marine looks; the crawler no longer makes a boss, since a boss must not start legless). Health, damage and size lists are unchanged.
- **Armour plates (`addPlates` in `gameRenderer.ts`):** dark metal boxes on the chest (`Spine2`) and both shoulders of a rigged marine boss, sized in metres against the rig's centimetre scale. Their placement on the bones is a guess; other models get none.
- **Limbs:** a boss loses its legs (and crawls) only once its health is under `dismember.bossCrawlHealth` (20%); a lost arm enrages it (speed x`bossEnrage` 1.3) instead of weakening its bite. Limbs still need `bossResist` times more wounds.
- **Head:** a head hit on a boss is capped at `hits.bossHeadMultiplier` (1.5). A head never bursts before death (it only does on the killing blow, as before).
- **Not done:** scars; the numbers are guesses.
