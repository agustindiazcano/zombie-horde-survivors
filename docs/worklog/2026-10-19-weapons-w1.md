# 2026-10-19 — Weapons W1: weapon state and a real weapon sheet

Branch `feat/weapon-instances` (cut from `main` after PR #49 to #51). 48 tests and all checks pass; nothing seen in a browser.

- **Weapon sheet:** in `data/items.json` weapons now carry real `fireMode` (`auto` or `semi`), `magazine`, `reloadTime`, `noise` and `accessorySlots`; only `weight` stays under `mocked`. The schema is in `src/sim/config.ts` (`FIRE_MODES`). `magazine` and `reloadTime` are optional (fists and the drone gun have none).
- **Weapon state instead of full instances:** items stay plain ids everywhere (hands, backpack stacks, crates, map loadouts), so no map or save needed a migration. The per-weapon state `{ loaded, accessories }` (`WeaponState` in `types.ts`) lives next to the id: `Stack.state` in the backpack and `Soldier.weaponStates` for the hands. `cellAt` / `putCell`, `moveItem`, `equipFromBackpack` and `unequipSlot` carry it between slots and units. A missing state means a fresh weapon with a full magazine (`handState` in `src/sim/weaponState.ts`).
- **Semi-automatic pistol:** `triggerPulled` (`weaponState.ts`) decides a pull: automatic guns fire while held and off cooldown; semi guns need a fresh click, which waits for the cooldown if it came early (`Soldier.triggerHeld` / `triggerQueued`). The AI is unchanged (it fires at the weapon's rate without clicking). Touch play sets `Input.autoTrigger` because its auto-aim holds the trigger, so semi guns keep firing there.
- **UI:** the weapon tooltip says the fire mode and shows the magazine and reload time (marked "no ammo rule yet").
- **Docs:** CONTROLS.md and README say the pistol fires once per click.
- Checked headless with throwaway tests (one shot per held click and again after release; the state moves from hand to backpack to hand and between hands), not committed.
- **Not done (next phases):** nothing spends the magazine yet (W2); `reloadTime` and `noise` have no rule; accessories cannot be fitted (W6); a crate holds plain ids, so stashing a weapon in a crate and taking it back resets it to a full magazine (fix in W2, when ammo matters); maps cannot yet set `loaded` or accessories on a placed weapon.
