# 2026-10-19 — Weapons W4: headshots and critical hits

Same branch as W1 to W3 and W5 (`feat/weapon-instances`, not merged). 48 tests and all checks pass; nothing seen in a browser.

- **Hit height (third and first person):** the player's input carries `aimLine` (the camera's ground position, height and slope, from `GameRenderer.sightLine()`); the controlled `Soldier` keeps it in `aimLine`. A shot lands on that line, so its height at the target says whether it crossed the head: the top `hits.headFraction` (20%) of the enemy's height (`hits.bodyHeight` 1.8 m x its size, or the enemy's own `height`, hound 1.0), up to 15% above. Hills are ignored (camera height is absolute).
- **Top-down and the AI:** no line, so a head hit is a chance: `hits.headChance` (6%) x 2 while aiming x (1 + 0.1 per weapon-skill level above 1), divided by (1 + 20 x aim error).
- **Critical hits:** `hits.critChance` (5%) plus 0.5% per skill level above 1, x`critMultiplier` 1.5. Head is x`headMultiplier` 2. **The higher multiplier wins, they never stack** (a tie goes to the head). A weapon item can override `headMultiplier`, `critChance` and `critMultiplier`. An enemy with `headshots: false` (the worm) has no head. Melee blows can crit but never hit the head.
- **Events:** `shot` and `melee` carry `hit: { kind: 'head' | 'crit' | 'body', damage } | null`; `zombieKilled` carries `hit`.
- **Feedback:** floating damage numbers (`src/damageNumbers.ts`: white body, orange head, yellow crit with "!"; only the controlled soldier's body hits are shown, everyone's head and crit hits), a crosshair glow on the controlled soldier's hits, and a ping (square for head, triangle for crit) in `audio.ts`.
- **Tests:** the shared test config sets `headChance`, `critChance` and `critSkillBonus` to 0 so damage stays exact. Checked headless with a throwaway test (head by height, body below, bigger multiplier wins, top-down chance), not committed.
- **Not done:** hills in the hit height; per-enemy head multipliers other than "none"; helmets; crit-only camera kick; a head hit marker in the squad screen; every number is an estimate.
