# 2026-10-21 — Parallax occlusion mapping, Map 11 texture lab, map list scroll

Branch `feat/helicopter` (not pushed, no PR). Lint, format, typecheck, build and the 48 tests pass. Unlike the other work, the shader was checked by rendering it in headless Chrome (a throw-away page, not kept): the carved bricks, stone blocks and riveted plates showed depth at normal and grazing angles and a sun shadow; the full game was not opened.

- **`src/render/pom.ts`:** four procedural materials (brick, stone, plates, cobble), drawn on a 512 px canvas each (albedo and height, tileable, no files, nothing downloaded). `createPomMaterial(kind)` is a `MeshStandardMaterial` patched with `onBeforeCompile`: it marches the view ray through the height field (up to 48 steps, fewer facing the surface), shifts the albedo lookup, darkens hollows, casts a soft self-shadow toward the first directional light (the sun) and tilts the normal by the slope. Tangents come from screen derivatives, so any mesh works. `pomBox` scales the box UVs so one texture repeat is `POM_TILE` = 2 m. `setPomQuality(0|1|2)` exists (off / 16 steps / 40 steps with shadow) but is not wired to the settings panel yet.
- **Props:** `pom_brick`, `pom_stone`, `pom_plates` (3.2 m walls) and `pom_cobble` (a 0.2 m slab soldiers step onto) in `neighbourhood.ts` (`buildProp`) and `PROP_HEIGHTS` in `config.ts`.
- **Map 11 "Texture lab"** (`scripts/gen-map11.py`): flat 80 x 80, a cobble slab, a far wall with one of each material, north-south walls on both sides, a corridor, stubs and pillars, four marines, no waves, third person, 09:00.
- **Time of day:** map rule `timeControl` (`logic.timeControl`, `GameConfig.timeControl`); in main.ts the **,** and **.** keys turn `timeOfDay` by 0.25 h and call `renderer.setEnvironment`. The hint line shows the clock.
- **Map list:** the menu list scrolls (`.menu__list--scroll`, max 50 vh) and maps sort with numeric order (Map 2 before Map 10).
- **Not done:** a POM option in Settings (Low / Medium / High), POM on the terrain itself (the ground is still flat vertex colours), real photographic textures (CC0 sets need the user's OK), roughness and metal maps, an azimuth control for the sun. Watch FPS with F3: the shader runs per pixel.
