# Plan: Celestial Event III (codename), a hide-and-seek demo for another game on this engine

Status: planned 2026-10-20, nothing built. Branch `feat/celestial-event-3`. It lives in this repo as **Map 6** while it is small; it moves to its own mode or repo when it grows. It replaces section 3 of [PLAN-test-maps.md](PLAN-test-maps.md) (the human vs human map becomes Map 7).

## 1. Story (the user's text, tidied)

In the 21st century the world's governments start a gradual **extraterrestrial declassification**: sightings are real, and there are unknown objects with apparently intelligent behaviour that defy known physics. They move at impossible G forces and leave none of the marks even science-fiction physics predicts: no sonic booms, no gravitational waves, no shock of light, no atmospheric heating (at their speed a normal object would release the energy of a nuclear blast). They could even open the door to new physics.

That the United States leads the disclosure is no accident: the information touches energy, economy, technology and defence of other nations. It does it because **something is coming**.

**UMBRA** is an agency that investigates these events and classifies them as **Celestial Events**. **Level IV** is when extraterrestrial vehicles interact directly with humans: scans, or physical harm. There are no large-scale attacks, out of fear that kilometre-long ships would deploy drones. Special weapons and protocols exist to handle these cases, censor them and eliminate possible biological or other contamination.

"Celestial Event III" is the **codename of the game/demo**; in the fiction the event is classified Level IV. (Ask the user if the codename and the level should match.)

### Lore addition: the Nodes (the user's concept, added 2026-10-20)

The planet was **terraformed** and humanity was **genetically modified** long ago. Every human carries a latent "node" rating from **1 to 10**: how much of an engineered, dormant capacity (telepathy, telekinesis, premonition) can wake up. Low nodes (1 to 3) are genetic noise, expendable; high nodes are the successful product of the modification.

This turns Level IV Celestial Events from random attacks into **maintenance and harvest operations**:

| Actor                      | What it does under this lore                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Metal spheres              | Vaporise low nodes (1 to 3): noise to clean up                                                    |
| Big ships (mother spheres) | Abduct selectively the high nodes that managed to awaken their latent abilities                   |
| Greys                      | The makers of the modification; they look for their successful product, the high nodes            |
| UMBRA                      | Predicts where the next Level IV event will land by mining the internet (see below); not a friend |

**The protagonist is a Node 10.** Greys and UMBRA both want them, so the player is the main target of both factions.

**UMBRA psychic OSINT.** UMBRA scans millions of daily posts on forums and social networks for keywords and descriptions of "living geometry" and shared nightmares. When a thousand people in different places dream the same shape or the same disaster, its model predicts where the next Level IV event falls. UMBRA wants the protagonist in a lab, as a human early-warning antenna, or dead if their mind is judged to be what attracts the kilometre-long ships.

### Lore addition: the cosmic layer (the user's concept, added 2026-10-20)

Nobody is good or evil: no one hates humanity, there are only conflicting survival interests at different scales. The only known fact is that what comes from deep space is **less convenient for us** than what is already here. Everything below is a hypothesis; the story should never confirm which one is true.

**Physics premise.** Space is a physical medium (a superfluid). The speed of light is the speed of sound of our local region of it, not a universal limit. The spheres move by **cavitation**: they open a vacuum bubble around themselves, which is why they have no inertia, no sonic boom and no atmospheric heating. The "cosmic edge" is only as far as light reaches before fading; beyond it the medium may be hundreds of billions of years old and unimaginably larger. This is the same layer the Nodes see as "living geometry": the tension and cavitation lines of the medium. A Node 10's powers are the biological ability to couple to that same medium.

**Nuclear weakness.** Spheres are vulnerable to nuclear detonations, which explains why sightings cluster around nuclear sites and why Roswell sits in a detonation zone. Gamma rays and fast neutrons cross the bubble wall, destabilise it, and the medium collapses back onto the hull like cavitation damage on a submarine, while neutrons pass through the hull and shred the occupants like subatomic shrapnel. The aliens watch silos to protect their navigation medium, not to protect us. (Not electromagnetic: the user rules out an EMP explanation.)

**The Antarctica incident.** China tested experimental weapons against spheres in Antarctica (emitters trying to reproduce "noise in the vacuum" without a nuclear blast). Effectiveness is ambiguous, sometimes yes, sometimes no: a hit only works if it lands in the exact resonance phase of the bubble, and when it does the sphere regains inertia at once and shatters on the ice at Mach 10. Western UMBRA is furious: the test raised extraterrestrial drone activity worldwide, and it is the direct trigger of why the game starts now. Human weapons and nukes also send longitudinal shock waves through the medium, faster than light, like a beacon for whatever is out there.

| Faction               | Strategy (all rational, none evil)                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Western UMBRA         | Low profile: do not provoke the system, let local harvests happen, sacrifice neighbourhoods, cover up, buy decades to understand vacuum physics. Infected people are culled |
| China (Antarctica)    | Active deterrence: waiting guarantees assimilation, so develop vacuum weapons now, whatever the noise. Prefers a small chance of surviving free to certain absorption       |
| The extraterrestrials | A local coalition (greys, spheres, big ships) that may be assimilated species working as one organism (a hive, symbionts, an AI, synthetics: left open). Possibly fleeing   |

**Symbolic contamination.** The "living geometry" behaves like a crystal seeded into a supersaturated solution: matter and neural networks are reorganised to copy its geometry. It must erase individuality and free will to run at full efficiency, which is why it formats rather than "attacks". Close contact with greys or a sphere scan starts rewriting a person's mind. This is the real reason for UMBRA's cleaning protocol: not a common virus, but a psychic and biological spread that makes people repeaters of the signal.

**What is coming.** The greys are not the top of the chain; they may be a local system frightened by something from beyond the light horizon, which explains why Level IV events accelerated: harvesting biomass, collecting high-capacity neural processors (Node 10s) and cleaning noise from the medium before it arrives. Nobody who entered its range sent data back, so only its distant effects are known.

| Name by source                                           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| UMBRA technical: "The Phase Front" / "Abyssal Attractor" | Background gravitational waves and vacuum tension shift from one region of the sky; it moves faster than its own light and displaces the apparent position of far galaxies |
| Node network: "The Black Tide" / "The Silence"           | Seen in dreams beyond the greys' fractals: a heavy mass of absolute geometry that eats the greys' telepathic network; where it touches, mental noise vanishes              |
| Hypothesis A: Vacuum Swarm                               | An intelligence that abandoned baryonic matter and encoded itself into vortices of the medium: a storm made of space                                                       |
| Hypothesis B: Systems Predator                           | An ancient mechanical-symbiotic ecosystem that feeds on civilisations discovering vacuum cavitation and follows their vibrations                                           |

**Late reveal for the player.** The protagonist starts out thinking the greys are the ultimate terror. When they telepathically link to one, they find it is **not hunting with arrogance: it is terrified of what is behind it**. The protagonist is also not a chosen hero but an organism with a useful mutation, pulled by three forces that each want a part of them: the system (a terminal ready for the geometry), Western UMBRA (a security risk and an intelligence asset to lock underground) and the Antarctica military line (a biological aiming system to read bubble phase). Open dilemma: is the Node 10 the key to hacking the spheres, or does waking their power already mean they were assimilated and now act as a beacon? The more they use their powers, the more they connect to the network (ties to the psychic signature mechanic below).

**Format note.** The user considers the material good enough for a short novel or miniseries (three acts: the signal, the harvest and the hunt, the awakening); the game is one expression of the same narrative bible.

### Opening scene (cinematic, no player input)

The player watches what looks like a **drone** floating over a US suburb. In the fiction this is **remote projection**: the Node 10 protagonist is asleep, projecting their consciousness, or having an exact premonition of what will happen in their own neighbourhood minutes before it happens. This also explains the camera during the blackout without a physical drone (the shielded UMBRA drone is dropped as an explanation). A night in a US suburb, outdoor lights on, neighbours looking at the stars.

| Beat | What happens                                                                                                                         | Sound / HUD                                                                                 |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Calm. Stars. Some stars start to move on trajectories and get closer                                                                 | Crickets, a dog; HUD: `ANOMALY DETECTED`                                                    |
| 2    | Blue and green glowing light orbs pass near the houses. Electric flicker, then a **blackout**                                        | Hum rising, then silence of the grid                                                        |
| 3    | Darkness; the orbs make formations in the sky. People scared: some run, some stand still                                             | HUD: `NO THERMAL SIGNATURE`                                                                 |
| 4    | Moonlight only. **Metal spheres** appear out of nowhere at impossible speed beside people's heads and fire a ray that vaporises them | Total silence while moving; a dry ionising crack on the kill; HUD: `CIVILIAN CASUALTIES: n` |
| 5    | People run harder; the spheres chase and vaporise everyone                                                                           | HUD: `NON-INTERVENTION PROTOCOL ACTIVE`                                                     |
| 6    | A **bigger sphere** abducts people with a beam of light and leaves                                                                   | HUD: `CLASSIFICATION: ESCALATING TO LEVEL IV`                                               |
| 7    | Another metal sphere lands; **grey** aliens step down. A man attacks one with a stick; a grey extends a **staff** and paralyses him  | The scene ends here for now                                                                 |

**Transition to gameplay.** The scene ends when a grey on the ground, or the big sphere, suddenly turns and "looks" straight at the player's invisible camera: they also operate on the telepathic plane and detect that a Node 10 is watching. The psychic impact wakes the protagonist in bed. At that moment the power in their real house cuts out and blue and green lights start showing through the window. They already know what is coming because they just "dreamed" it, and the **hide and seek** starts.

This replaces the earlier open question of who the player controls (section 7, question 1): the player is the Node 10 neighbour, inside their own house.

### Mechanics for a Node 10 (stealth plus powers with a cost)

Powers must be dangerous to use, or the player feels like a superhero and the terror is lost.

| Mechanic                         | How it works                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Psychic signature (the risk)     | Metal spheres and the blue / green orbs do not hunt body heat but **anomalous neural activity**. Using telekinesis (move an object, make noise in another room to distract a grey) creates a "spike"; using it a lot pulls the seeker orbs straight to the player                                            |
| Short-term premonition (stealth) | Instead of a radar, the player closes their eyes and concentrates to see translucent "echoes" (silhouettes) of where a grey will walk in the next 5 seconds, to plan a hiding spot without leaning out                                                                                                       |
| Living geometry and telepathy    | When a grey is near, the screen edges distort with fractal geometric patterns. The mind involuntarily translates their transmissions: the player hears their "orders" or sees flashes of what the grey sees. Useful to know if they are about to be found, but it floods the screen if the grey is too close |

These come after E6 and need interiors (E8). The first playable level is inside the player's house.

### Threat roles for the playable part (from the user's story)

| Threat                    | Role         | Behaviour                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Light orbs (blue / green) | Seekers      | Drift, pass through walls and windows looking for signs of life; they **mark** whoever they see. Staying still / crouching / cover reduces being seen |
| Metal spheres             | Executioners | When a light orb marks a target a sphere appears beside it with no warning and vaporises it. Silent until the kill                                    |
| Greys                     | Slow hunters | Walk through houses searching for people to take alive; the staff paralyses. They reuse the hunter AI (sight, lose track, search)                     |
| UMBRA                     | Second fear  | Not the good side: it hunts the Node 10 to put them in a lab, use them as an early-warning antenna, or eliminate them (see the Nodes lore above)      |

## 3. New sim and render pieces

| Piece             | Where                                 | Notes                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Civilians         | `src/sim/civilians.ts` (from animals) | Unarmed; states `idle`, `watch`, `panic` (run away from the nearest threat), `freeze`, `paralysed`, `abducted`. Counter for the HUD                               |

| Greys             | enemy in `game.json` + hunter AI      | Slow, search sweeps; `staff` attack applies `paralysed` for N seconds instead of damage                                                                           |
| Event script      | `src/sim/events.ts`                   | A timeline in the map JSON (`script: [{ t, do }]`): `starsMove`, `lightsFlicker`, `blackout`, `spawnOrb`, `hud`, `camera`; deterministic, driven by the sim clock |
| Cinematic camera  | `src/cameraControls.ts`               | A `scripted` mode: drone path keyframes, no input, letterbox; ends by handing control over                                                                        |
| Sky               | `src/render/sky.ts`                   | Star points; a subset follows trajectories toward the camera; orbs as emissive spheres with a point light                                                         |
| Blackout and moon | `gameRenderer.ts`                     | `lighting` rule per map: lamps on / off / flickering, moon intensity, no flashlights for civilians                                                                |
| HUD readout       | `src/hud.ts`                          | A cold clinical text overlay (`ANOMALY DETECTED`, `CIVILIAN CASUALTIES: n`...)                                                                                    |
| Sound             | `src/audio.ts`                        | Procedural: crickets, grid hum, ionising crack, silence on movement. No new files                                                                                 |

All numbers go in `data/game.json` (`orbs`, `civilians`, `events`) with the zod schema in `src/sim/config.ts`; the shared test config in `tests/sim/helpers.ts` gets the new required sections. No new tests (the user's rule); the 48 stay green.
